Runefall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Runefall is a single-player game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Information we collect

Runefall does not run any server of its own and does not collect, sell or share personal information. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The only online features are the optional Google Play Games features described below, which are handled by Google.

Game saves and settings

Your characters, progress, achievements and settings are saved on your own device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the local copy (unless you use the optional cloud save below).

Google Play Games (optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses Google's Play Games Services to (1) store a cloud copy of your saved characters, stash and achievements in your own Google account so you can restore them after reinstalling, and (2) post your best Breach depth and character level, with your character class and your Play Games name, to a public leaderboard that other players can see. This data is stored and processed by Google and is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive it on a server of our own. If you do not sign in, nothing is sent anywhere. You can sign out or manage your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app and your Google account settings.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases (Cat Companion and Expanded Stash) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play only tells the game which items you own, so it can unlock them. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Internet and permissions

The game runs offline. It only connects to Google Play for purchases and, if you choose to sign in, for Play Games cloud save and the leaderboard. It does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

Runefall is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Use the developer contact email listed on Runefall's Google Play page.
